Pricing

What You'll Pay - No Hidden Costs

Replacing the sealed unit is a fraction of the cost of a new window, and on a sound frame it is the sensible job. Price comes down to the size of the unit, how many you need doing and the glass specification. Toughened glass is required in some positions by the regulations, obscure glass costs a little more than clear, and a leaded or Georgian bar unit costs more again because of the way it is made up.

We survey free of charge and check the frame and the seals before quoting, because there is no point putting new glass into a frame that is on its way out. If a swap is genuinely worth doing we will tell you, and if it is not, we will tell you that too rather than take the work.

Unit size

Glass is priced by area, so a large picture pane costs more to replace than a small casement.

How many units

Doing several in one visit is cheaper per unit than a single call out, because the labour and the delivery are shared across them.

Glass specification

Clear, obscure, toughened, laminated, leaded or bar units all differ in price. Some positions require toughened or laminated by regulation.

Condition of the frame

A sound frame is a straight swap. A frame with failed gaskets or damaged beading needs parts, and a frame that is failing needs replacing instead.

Free site survey and free delivery, priced in writing after we have measured up. What is covered is set out at the survey, so nothing is left to be assumed.

How We Work

What To Expect

A glazing swap is usually quick and undramatic, which is exactly what you want. Here is how it goes.

1

Free survey

We measure each failed unit and check the frame, the beads and the drainage. Misting is a symptom, and sometimes the cause is a frame problem that new glass will not fix.

2

Written price

A written figure per unit and for the visit, covering the glass, delivery and fitting. No call out fee for the survey.

3

Making and delivery

Units are made to your measured sizes. We confirm the lead time in writing and ring you when the glass lands with us. Delivery is free.

4

Fitting day

Beads off, old unit out, new unit packed and set on the correct bridge packers, beads back and everything cleaned down. The old glass leaves with us.
