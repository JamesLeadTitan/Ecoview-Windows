Pricing

What You'll Pay - No Hidden Costs

Vertical sliders cost more than a standard casement of the same size, and that is down to the mechanism. A sash window has balances, guides and two moving sashes to make and set up, so there is more in it than a hinged window. Size, the bar layout on the glass and the finish all move the price from there. Astragal bars and a woodgrain finish look right on a period frontage and add to the cost.

We survey each opening free of charge and price it in writing, including the window, delivery, fitting, sealing and the making good. If your property is listed or sits in a conservation area, tell us at the survey, because what is allowed varies and it is better dealt with before anything is ordered.

Size and sash count

Larger sashes need heavier balances, and a triple sash arrangement is more work to make and set up than a standard pair.

Bar layout

Plain glass is the cheapest. Georgian or astragal bars look right on a period house and add to the price per window.

Finish

White is the value option. Woodgrain and heritage colours cost more and are usually what makes the window look correct on an older frontage.

The existing opening

Old timber sash boxes often hide rot or movement. We check at the survey and tell you what putting it right involves.

How We Work

What To Expect

Sliders take a little more setting up than a casement, and that is time well spent. Here is the process.

1

Free survey

We measure each opening, look at the existing box and cill for rot or movement, and go through bar layouts and finishes against the rest of the frontage so the windows look right rather than nearly right.

2

Written price

One written figure covering the windows, delivery, fitting, sealing and making good, with each opening priced so you can see the detail.

3

Making and delivery

Sliders are made to your surveyed sizes with the balances set for the weight of the sashes. We confirm the lead time in writing. Delivery is free.

4

Fitting and setting up

Old window out, new frame packed and fixed, then the sashes hung and the balances adjusted until they run smoothly and hold at any height. We take the old frames away and make good inside and out.
