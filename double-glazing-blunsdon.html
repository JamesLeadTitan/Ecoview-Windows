Blunsdon

Local Double Glazing You Can Trust

Blunsdon sits up on the ridge north of Swindon, and anyone living there knows what the wind does. Exposure is the reason draughty windows get noticed sooner in Blunsdon than they do down in the town, and it is why the perimeter seal and the fixing matter as much as the glass specification when a window is replaced. A frame that is foamed in and left is a frame that will whistle by the second winter.

The housing runs from older stone properties in Broad Blunsdon to newer detached and estate homes closer to the A419. We survey each opening in person, check the reveals and the cills, and pack and seal the frames properly on fitting day. Free site survey, free delivery, and a written price before anything is ordered.
