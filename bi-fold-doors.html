Pricing

What You'll Pay - No Hidden Costs

Bi-folds are priced by the metre and by the number of leaves, so the opening you have decides most of the cost before anything else is chosen. A three leaf set into an existing opening is a very different job to a five leaf run that needs the wall opening up and a lintel putting in. The frame material matters too. Aluminium holds slimmer sightlines over a wide span, uPVC comes in cheaper on smaller openings.

We survey the opening free of charge, check what is holding the wall up above it, and give you a written price covering the doors, the delivery, the fitting, the threshold and the clear up. If the job needs structural work before the doors can go in, we tell you at the survey so you can plan it, rather than finding out on fitting day.

Width and number of leaves

Every extra leaf adds glass, hinges and running gear. A wider set also needs a heavier track, which is where the cost climbs.

Aluminium or uPVC

Aluminium takes bigger spans with thinner frames, so you see more glass. uPVC is the better value choice on modest openings.

Threshold and floor level

A low threshold looks better and trips no one, but it needs the floor levels and the drainage to work. We check that at the survey.

Opening up the wall

If you want wider than what is there now, that is structural work with a lintel. It is a real cost and we will not pretend otherwise.

How We Work

What To Expect

Bi-folds are a bigger job than a straight window swap, so the running order matters. This is how we do it.

1

Free survey

We measure the opening, check the head above it, look at the floor levels for the threshold and talk through leaf configuration. Which way the doors stack and where the traffic door sits changes how you use the room, so it is worth getting right.

2

Written price

One price covering the doors, the track, the threshold, delivery, fitting and taking the old unit away. Any structural work is listed separately so you can see exactly what is what.

3

Making and delivery

Bi-folds are made to your measurements, so there is a lead time. We confirm it in writing and ring you the moment the set arrives with us. Delivery is free.

4

Fitting and setting up

The frame is fixed and packed, the leaves are hung and then the whole run is adjusted so it folds and locks smoothly. We show you how to work it before we leave and take the old doors away.
