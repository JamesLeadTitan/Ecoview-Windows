Pricing

What You'll Pay - No Hidden Costs

Blinds are priced by size, by type and by whether they sit inside the sealed unit or in front of it. Integral blinds live between the panes, so they never need dusting and cannot be pulled about by children or pets, but they have to be specified when the glass is made. Conventional blinds fitted to the frame or the reveal cost less and can be changed later, which suits some rooms better.

We measure free of charge and price in writing. If we are already fitting your windows or doors, saying so at the survey usually works out better for you, because integral blinds can go into the units while they are being made rather than being an afterthought.

Integral or fitted

Integral blinds sit inside the sealed unit and are specified with the glass. Conventional blinds fit to the frame or reveal and cost less.

Size and number of openings

Wider openings need wider blinds and more of them. Doing a whole room in one visit is better value than one at a time.

Type and operation

Venetian, pleated or roller, and manual or motorised. Operation is what moves the price most once the size is fixed.

Colour and finish

Standard colours are the value option. Matching a specific frame colour or finish adds to the cost.

Free site survey and free delivery, priced in writing after we have measured up. What is covered is set out at the survey, so nothing is left to be assumed.

How We Work

What To Expect

Blinds are a measuring job first and foremost. Get that wrong and nothing else matters.

1

Free survey

We measure each opening and check how the window operates, because a blind that fouls a handle or an opener is no use. If the windows are ours, we already know the frame detail.

2

Written price

A written figure covering the blinds, delivery and fitting, priced per opening so you can pick and choose room by room.

3

Making and delivery

Blinds are made to your measured sizes. We confirm the lead time in writing and delivery to us is free.

4

Fitting

Brackets set, blinds hung, operation checked at every opening and any packaging taken away with us. We leave the reveals clean.
