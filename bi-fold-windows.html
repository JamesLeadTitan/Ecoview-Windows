Pricing

What You'll Pay - No Hidden Costs

A bi-fold window is priced on the width of the opening and the number of panes that have to fold into it. A two pane servery over a kitchen worktop is at the modest end. A four pane run across a garden room wall costs more, because the frames carry more glass and need heavier hardware to keep folding smoothly for years rather than months.

We survey the opening free of charge and price it properly, including the sill, the fitting and the making good inside and out. If your worktop or bar run has to line through with the sill, that gets measured at the survey rather than assumed, because a servery that sits an inch proud of the counter is a job nobody enjoys looking at.

Width and pane count

More panes means more hinges and more weight on the frame, so the price moves with the number of folds, not just the metres.

Frame material

Aluminium keeps the frames slim on a wide servery. uPVC is the better value option on a smaller kitchen opening.

Sill and worktop detail

Running a sill flush with a worktop or bar top is neat but takes more setting out. It is worth deciding before we order.

Access and what is there now

A ground floor opening in sound brickwork is straightforward. Rotten timber or a distorted opening adds preparation time.

How We Work

What To Expect

Bi-fold windows are a small job with fiddly setting out, so the survey does most of the work. Here is the running order.

1

Free survey

We measure the opening, check the sill line against your worktop or bar, and talk through which way the panes should stack so they are not in the way when the window is open.

2

Written price

One figure covering the window, delivery, fitting, sealing and the clear up. No call out fee for the survey and no separate delivery charge bolted on later.

3

Making and delivery

The window is made to your measurements. We confirm the lead time in writing and ring you when it arrives with us. Delivery to us is free.

4

Fitting day

Old unit out, new frame packed, fixed and sealed, then the panes hung and adjusted until they fold and latch cleanly. We take the old frame and the packaging with us.
